### Farmer briefing to confirm

- [ ] Farmer understands that a harvest estimate is not automatically an offer.
- [ ] Farmer chooses how much of the harvest to offer through AGRI-CI.
- [ ] Farmer understands that an accepted commitment reserves quantity for an aggregation/order.
- [ ] Actual received weight is recorded by an authorized collection agent, not self-declared as final weight by the farmer.
- [ ] Quality grading may split received produce into different lots/grades.
- [ ] Settlement is based on delivered and eligible quantity, not merely announced quantity.
- [ ] Price and authorized deductions are explained before settlement.
- [ ] Farmer knows whom to contact for a quantity, quality or payment dispute.

## 10. Operating principle

The first pilot must optimize for **traceability, reconciliation and learning**, not transaction volume. Increase volume only after completed orders reconcile from farmer allocation through final payment.
