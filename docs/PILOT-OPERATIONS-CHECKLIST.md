# AGRI-CI Controlled Commercial Pilot Checklist

Before accepting a real order:
- farmer identity/profile verified by pilot operator;
- buyer identity and contact verified;
- product, quality, quantity, delivery window and price explicitly confirmed;
- collection point and responsible agent assigned;
- transporter and vehicle assigned;
- payment provider account and webhook tested in sandbox;
- dispute/support contact communicated to participants;
- consent/privacy notice accepted;
- daily operational reconciliation owner assigned.

Before paying a farmer:
- collection weight recorded;
- quality check attached to the same order;
- lot traceability complete;
- delivery status verified according to pilot settlement policy;
- deductions visible and authorized;
- payment initiated only through the licensed provider integration;
- ledger and provider reference retained.

The software must not be used as evidence that a financial institution, transporter,
buyer or farmer has been independently certified by AGRI-CI.
